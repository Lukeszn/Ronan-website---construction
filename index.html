Concrete cutting services

Diamond cutting leaves a clean, accurate edge without the vibration and cracking of breaking concrete by hand. Each service shows the typical limit, so you can see what fits your job.

Wall sawing

A track-mounted saw cuts straight, square openings through concrete and block, ready for frames and lintels.

  • Door and window openings
  • Lift shafts and service openings
  • Lowering or removing walls
Cut depthup to 730 mm

Floor and road sawing

Walk-behind saws for slabs, yards, roads and footpaths. Straight cuts for trenches, drains and joints.

  • Drainage and service trenches
  • Expansion and control joints
  • Tarmac and asphalt reinstatement
Cut depthup to 400 mm

Core drilling

Round holes drilled clean through walls and floors for pipes, ducts and cables, with no damage around them.

  • Soil pipes, flues and extract fans
  • Electrical and data ducting
  • Angled and overhead coring
Hole size12 to 500 mm

Wire sawing

A diamond wire loops around the section to cut heavily reinforced or very thick concrete a blade cannot reach.

  • Beams, columns and bridge decks
  • Thick foundations and plinths
  • Low-vibration removal
Cut depthno practical limit

Controlled demolition

Cut, lift and break out concrete in a planned order, so the rest of the structure stays sound.

  • Slab and stair removal
  • Breaking out bases and footings
  • Strip-out for renovations
Methodcut, lift, break

Chasing and hand sawing

Hand-held and ring saws for tight spaces, plus wall chasing for cables and pipes in concrete and block.

  • Wall chasing for electrics
  • Cutting in confined spaces
  • Kerbs, steps and lintels
Cut depthup to 260 mm

Common questions

Anything else, send a message or ring 087 123 4567.

Can you price the job without a site visit?

Often, yes. For smaller jobs like core holes or a single opening, photos plus the thickness and rough size are usually enough for a fixed price. Larger or awkward jobs get a site visit first.

Do I need an engineer to cut an opening in a load-bearing wall?

Yes. Openings in load-bearing walls are cut to your engineer's design, with propping in place before cutting starts. If you do not have an engineer yet, ask and I can tell you what they will need to know.

How much dust and mess will there be?

Very little dust. Cutting is done wet, or with on-tool extraction, to keep silica dust down. The water leaves a slurry, which is cleaned up before I leave, and the area is protected first.

Can you work evenings or weekends?

Yes, by arrangement. Out-of-hours cutting suits shops, offices and live sites where noise needs to be kept away from working hours.

What happens to the cut concrete?

Cut sections can be lifted out and stacked on site, or broken out ready for removal. This is agreed in the quote, so you know what to expect on the day.
